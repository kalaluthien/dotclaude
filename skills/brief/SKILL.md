# Brief

Summarize the named subject in bullets: conclusions first, then the action-related information. No preamble, and no details unless asked.

Then route the deliverable by its lifetime. An answer that dies with the session stays chat text. A one-off visual review goes to a disposable HTML under the session scratchpad. Durable knowledge goes to a committed `docs/` spec (`.md`) or view (`.html`).
