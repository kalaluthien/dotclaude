# show-me

Show the topic, the one named or else the conversation's, as a document: a
heading, then what the reader needs in the order they need it, as prose,
tables or lists as the material asks. A blank is not shown: hand the document
to `grill-me`.

Deliver it where the ask says, else in chat as markdown.
